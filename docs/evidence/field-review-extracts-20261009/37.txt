L132: ## Get to know Surface Studio 2
L133: 
L134: Surface Studio 2 has powerful performance designed to help you create, work, and more.
L135: 
L136: ### Volume and Power buttons
L137: 
L138: cite76†Image: SurfaceStudio-diagram-side_en L139: ### Ports and audio
L140: 
L141: Surface Studio 2 comes with various ports that can be found at the back of the base:
L142: 
L143:   * 1 High power USB 3.0 port
L144:   * 3 additional USB 3.0 ports
L145:   * 1 Ethernet port
L146:   * Power port for included power cable
L147:   * 1 USB-C port
L148:   * 1 SDXC card reader
L149:   * 1 auxiliary audio in/out port
L150: 
L151: Note
L152: When you unplug the power cable, unplug it from the wall first. The plug that’s attached to the base has a lock to keep it in place when you move your Surface Studio 2. If you need to unplug the power cable from the base, slide the plug away from the base to release the lock.
L153: ### Cameras
L154: 
L155: Surface Studio 2 has a 1080p camera and a Windows Hello sign-in camera.
L156: 
L157: cite77†Image: A picture of the Surface Studio display, with labels identifying the position of the two cameras near the center at the top L158: 
L159: Note
L160: 
L161: The serial number for your Surface Studio 2 is located on the bottom of the base.
L162: 
L163: ## Surface Studio 2 features
L164: ### A brilliant screen for your ideas
L165: 
L166: cite78†Image: Surface Studio 2 screen L167: 
L168: Surface Studio 2 has a 28-inch, adjustable PixelSense^{TM} Display with 13.5 million pixels to bring your ideas, images, and drawings to life. Touch it. Draw on it. Visualize and develop your ideas like you would on a sheet of paper.
L169: ### Transforms from Desktop to Studio
L170: 
L171: Whether you’re sketching your next great idea, collaborating with a team, or pitching your work, Surface Studio 2 is ready to adapt to wherever you are in the creative process.
L172: 
L173: cite79†Image: Surface Studio 2 transforming from Desktop to Studio Mode L174: 
L175: Hold the side of the screen and use the Zero Gravity Hinge to transform Surface Studio 2 from Desktop to Studio Mode. It’s the perfect height to write, draw, or doodle on your screen.
L176: When you’re ready for a desktop again, lift the screen back up.
L177: ### Professional-grade power and performance
L178: 
L179: cite80†Image: Photo of Surface Studio, with Surface Mouse and Keyboard L180: 
L181: Surface Studio 2 is up for anything. Our most powerful Surface yet, it has an Intel® Core™ i7 processor and a discrete NVIDIA® GeForce® GPU.  You can run professional-grade software like Adobe Premiere Pro, Solidworks, and Autodesk, and use unique tools like Surface Pen and Surface Dial to reinvent the way you create.
L182: In addition to Surface Pen, Surface Studio 2 comes with a Bluetooth mouse and keyboard. They’re pre-paired and ready to go whenever you are.
L183: ### Sign in with Windows Hello
L184: 
L185: cite81†Image: Start screen with Windows Hello greeting L186: 
L187: Say goodbye to your password. The front-facing infrared camera on your Surface Studio 2 is ready to use with Windows Hello – it’s faster and more secure than typing a password. Once it’s set up, your Surface Studio 2 will recognize your face, and only yours, almost instantly.
L188: 
L189: ## Make it your own
L190: ### Write and draw naturally with the newest Surface Pen
L191: We all think differently when we’re holding a pen. With 4,096 pressure points that respond to your touch, tilt for shading, and a natural-feeling rubber eraser, the newest Surface Pen and Surface Studio 2 work together to harness that change in perspective. Click the top of the Pen to launch Windows Ink Workspace, a place where you can take notes, sketch, and draw on your screen. Surface Pen attaches magnetically to the side of the display, so it’s always there when you need it.
L192: ### Highest quality mouse and keyboard
L193: 
L194: Design is in the details, and it’s no different for the Surface Keyboard and Surface Mouse.
L195: 
L196: Surface Keyboard is built with a strong, soft-finished aluminum top case, and it pairs automatically with your Surface Studio 2 without wires getting in the way. Typing is fast, quiet, and responsive.
L197: 
L198: cite82†Image: Surface Keyboard L199: To change keyboard settings, select the Start cite83†Image: icon for Windows logo icon button, and then select Settings > Devices > Typing.
L200: Surface Mouse is also wireless, with a 24-detent metal scroll wheel. Click and scrolling sounds were crafted to be quiet enough not to disturb, but distinct enough to hear.
L201: 
L202: cite84†Image: Surface Mouse L203: 
L204: To change mouse settings, select the Start cite83†Image: icon for Windows logo icon button, and then select Settings > Devices > Mouse.
L205: ### Surface Dial: A tool for the creative process
L206: 
L207: cite85†Image: meet-car-img8_en L208: 
L209: Surface Dial is a new way to interact with your Surface Studio 2 – change drawing tools like ink color and thickness without interrupting your creative flow.
L210: 
L211: Surface Dial is an optional accessory. Get one at a Microsoft Store near you.
L212: 
L213: Want to know more? cite86†Meet Surface Dial .
