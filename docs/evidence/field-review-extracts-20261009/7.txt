L161: You can charge your Surface using USB-C or Surface Connect. For more information, see cite80†How to charge Surface. L162: ## Surface Pro 7+ with LTE Advanced
L163: 
L164: Some models of Surface Pro 7+ support an LTE connection. To check if your Surface Pro 7+ supports a SIM card, here's what to do:
L165: 
L166:   1. Place the screen of your Surface Pro 7+ on a table, then lift the kickstand.
L167: 
L168:   2. On the left side of the Surface near the hinge, you'll see one of the following:
L169:     1. SIM tray
L170: The tray is inserted into the device and requires the SIM ejector tool to remove.
L171:     2. microSD card slot
L172: An exposed slot where you can slide a microSD card and click it into place.

L274:   * Surface Pro 7+ battery life: Up to 15 hours of battery life based on typical Surface device usage. Testing conducted by Microsoft in November 2020 (Surface Pro 7+ with Intel 11th Gen Intel® Core™ i5-1135G7 @ 2.40GHz , 256GB SSD, 8GB DRAM) using preproduction software and preproduction configurations of Surface Pro 7+. Testing consisted of full battery discharge with a mixture of active use and modern standby.
L275: The active use portion consists of (1) a web browsing test accessing 8 popular websites over multiple open tabs, (2) a productivity test utilizing Microsoft Word, PowerPoint, Excel and Outlook, and (3) a portion of time with the device in use with idle applications. Tested with Windows Version 10.0.19041.1(20H1) and Edge version 85.0.564.51. All settings were default except screen brightness was set to 150nits with Auto-Brightness disabled. Wi-Fi was connected to a network.
L276: Battery life varies significantly with settings, usage and other factors.
L277:   * LTE battery life: Up to 13.5 hours of battery life based on typical Surface device usage. Testing conducted by Microsoft in November 2020 (Surface Pro 7+ with Intel 11th Gen Intel® Core™ i5-1135G7 @ 2.40GHz , 256GB SSD, 8GB DRAM) using preproduction software and preproduction configurations of Surface Pro 7+. Testing consisted of full battery discharge with a mixture of active use and modern standby.
L278: The active use portion consists of (1) a web browsing test accessing 8 popular websites over multiple open tabs, (2) a productivity test utilizing Microsoft Word, PowerPoint, Excel and Outlook, and (3) a portion of time with the device in use with idle applications. All settings were default except screen brightness was set to 150nits with Auto-Brightness disabled. LTE enabled and device connected to LTE Network. Wi-Fi and Bluetooth in battery mode.
L279: Battery life varies significantly with settings, usage and other factors.
