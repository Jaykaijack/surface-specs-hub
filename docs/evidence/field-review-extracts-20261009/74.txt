L157: 
L158: Surface Studio 2+ comes with Windows 11 Pro edition.
L159: ## Highlighted Surface Studio 2+ features
L160:   * Be productive throughout your day on the brilliant and large 28" PixelSense™ touchscreen—lower and raise the display easily with the Zero Gravity Hinge to work your best at any angle.
L161:   * Multitask using the powerful 11th Gen Intel® Core™ H-35 processor. Make calls, edit photos and presentations, and tackle your other projects with confidence.
L162:   * The NVIDIA® GeForce RTX 3060 dGPU is built with the latest RT Cores, Tensor Cores, and streaming multiprocessors for great performance. Whether you’re drawing, editing photos, or taking a break to play the latest Xbox games when you have Xbox Game Pass Ultimate ^{1} and an Xbox controller ^{2} paired with it, you’re covered.
L163:   * Let your ideas flow on the immersive touchscreen with Surface Pen ^{3}. Collaborate together or remotely on a digital whiteboard using touch or your pen.
L164:   * Connect with people on video calls on the large, crisp display with a 1080p HD camera and dual far-field Studio Mics that capture your voice clearly and reduce background noise.
L165:   * Surface Studio 2+ has a variety of ports, including USB-C with Thunderbolt™ 4. Transfer large files fast with an external hard drive and pair with a range of accessories.
L166:   * Enjoy spectacular picture quality with Dolby Vision® ^{4} and immersive cinematic sound with Dolby Atmos®.^{5}
