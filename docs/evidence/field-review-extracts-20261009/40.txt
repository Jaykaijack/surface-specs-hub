L122: 
L123: Applies To
L124:

L127: Note
L128: 
L129: This device has reached its end-of-servicing date based on the cite75†Surface Lifecycle for Android-based devices†learn.microsoft.com and will no longer receive newer Android version and security updates.
L130: With two screens, Surface Duo helps you get more done in better ways. It’s a thin and light mobile device that folds and fits in your pocket, so you can always take it with you. Just open your Surface Duo to use the dual, high-resolution PixelSense™ Fusion Displays that give you 8.1” of space to work on the go throughout your day—whether it’s for work, school, or yourself.

L144: ## The Android you know—with your favorite Microsoft apps built in
L145: Surface Duo combines the Google Android^{TM} operating system with the best Microsoft 365 apps—including apps like Microsoft Office, Outlook, Microsoft Teams, OneDrive, Microsoft Edge, and more. Since it runs on Android, you can download any other apps and games you want from the Google Play Store. This combination helps you stay connected to people and be more productive, wherever you are. (Some apps and features may require a wireless plan, additional subscriptions, or fees.)
L146: ## Two screens that adapt to you
L147: 
L148: Surface Duo has a revolutionary 360° hinge, so you can fold it however you want. It adapts to you. Hold it like a book and have two apps open side-by-side with one on each screen. No need to switch back and forth between two apps anymore.
L149: 
L150: Some apps are made for two screens. When you want more room to type, work, or browse your favorite sites, just drag one of these apps to the middle, then rotate your Surface Duo to enjoy the extra space.
L151: Ready to talk on the phone or take pictures? Fold one screen back. And when it's time to watch your favorite shows or chat on a video call, just fold your Surface Duo like a tent, relax, and enjoy.
L152: 
L153: To learn more, see cite77†Different ways to use Surface Duo and open Tips on your Surface Duo.
L154: 
L155: cite78†Video Player†learn-video.azurefd.net L156: ## Get the most from Surface Duo with apps made for two screens
L157: Some apps are already installed and made for two screens, such as Microsoft Office, Outlook, OneNote, OneDrive, and Microsoft Teams (just to name a few). Just drag the app to the middle and get a whole new view. For example, you’ll see an overview on one side with the details on the other. You don’t have to go back and forth in an app to get the info you need. For a detailed list of apps that are made for two screens, see cite77†Different ways to use Surface Duo .
