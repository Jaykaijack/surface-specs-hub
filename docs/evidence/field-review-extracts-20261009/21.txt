L163: ## Surface Laptop 2 features
L164: ### Beautifully designed and crafted
L165: 
L166: Surface Laptop 2 complements your personal style and it's all about beautiful details—right down to a design that lets you effortlessly open your laptop with one hand. The soft palm rest, responsive keyboard, and large cite82†touchpad bring typing comfort to a whole new level. Plus, the Signature Alcantara® fabric on the keyboard and palm rest is stain-resistant, wears beautifully, and is specially treated for easy cite83†cleaning .
L167: ### Portable and powerful
L168: 
L169: You don’t have to choose between the power to do what you want and traveling light. Surface Laptop 2 comes with an 8th-generation Intel® Core™ processor that’ll breeze through any task, and a battery that’s powerful enough to last all day. And at just 2.7 pounds, Surface Laptop 2 fits easily in your bag without weighing you down.
L170: ### A stunning screen
L171: 
L172: The 13.5” PixelSense™ display with cite82†touchscreen has razor-sharp resolution for photos, videos, reports, and presentations. And the edge-to-edge display means more space for your ideas.
