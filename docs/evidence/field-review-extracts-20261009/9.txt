L150:

L153: ### Runs professional grade software and apps
L154: 
L155: Surface Pro runs the programs you need for work, and the high-resolution PixelSense^{™} Display brings your ideas to life. And, when it’s time to relax, Surface Pro runs the apps and games you love.

L186: cite90†Image: one-slim-browse-xbox-design-lab-s L187: ## Work however you want, wherever you want
L188: 
L189: Surface Pro 6 is slim and light, so it’s easy to take wherever you’re headed. Surface Pro 6 has a hinged Kickstand that adjusts to where you are and what you’re doing, whether you’re on a flight, relaxing on the beach, or creating in the studio.
L190: 
L191: ### Laptop Mode
L192: 
L193: cite91†Image: Surface Pro 6 with type cover and a Surface Pen L194: 
L195: Transform Surface Pro 6 into Laptop Mode by opening the built-in Kickstand and adding a removable Surface Pro Signature Type Cover.
L196: ### Studio Mode
L197: 
L198: cite92†Image: Image of Surface Pro 6 in studio mode with Surface Pen next to it L199: 
L200: Lower the Kickstand to put Surface Pro 6 into Studio Mode – it’s the perfect writing and drawing angle where you can create with optional accessories like Surface Pen and Surface Dial.
L201: ### Tablet Mode
L202: 
L203: cite93†Image: Image of Surface Pro 6 in tablet mode L204: 
L205: With the Kickstand closed, Surface Pro 6 is a powerful tablet that’s light and portable. Use it with Surface Pen to take notes, jot down an idea, or draw. Or simply relax at the end of the day with your favorite movie.
L206: ### Exceptional power and performance
L207: 
L208: cite94†Image: Image of a Surface Pro 6 on a desk L209: 
L210: Surface Pro 6 is light enough to slip under your arm and off to your next meeting, but has an a 8th-generation Intel® Core™ processor with the power and performance to run all the full desktop software you need to get things done.
L211: 
L212: ### Collaborate in video calls
L213: 
L214: The front-facing Skype HD camera that Surface Pro 6 has is perfect for video meetings and calls. cite95†Get Skype at Microsoft Store†www.microsoft.com .
L215: ### Do things faster with touch
L216: 
L217: Surface Pro 6 has a stunning high-resolution 12.3” PixelSense^{™} Display, which brings your ideas to life and responds quickly to your touch. To find out about touchscreen or touchpad gestures, see cite96†Touch gestures for Windows .
L218: ### Leave the power cord at home
L219: 
L220: With up to 13.5 hours of video playback*, you don’t need to worry about running out of power or bringing a charger with you. To change your battery settings, see cite97†Battery saving tips .
L221: * Up to 13.5 hours of video playback. Testing conducted by Microsoft in April 2017 using preproduction Intel Core i5, 256GB, 8 GB RAM device. Testing consisted of full battery discharge during video playback. All settings were default except: Wi-Fi was associated with a network and Auto-Brightness disabled. Battery life varies significantly with settings, usage, and other factors.
L222: ## Get things done with Windows, Microsoft Office, and OneDrive
L223: 
L224: Surface Pro 6 runs the Windows you know and trust—and Microsoft Office, including Word, Excel, and PowerPoint, for producing your best work. And OneDrive is included, so your files are stored safely in the cloud. Check out cite98†Windows help to learn more.
