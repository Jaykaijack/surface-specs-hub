L158: ## Surface Pro 3 features
L159: ### Work your way
L160: 
L161: Flip out the Surface Pro 3 kickstand to any angle and work or play comfortably at your desk, on the couch, or while giving a hands-free presentation. Choose the angle that's right for you. Multi-touch lets you use your fingers to select, zoom, or move things around on the screen (learn about cite79†using a touchscreen ).
L162: 
L163: Surface Pro 3 supports standard Wi-Fi protocols (802.11a/b/g/n/ac) and Bluetooth®, and it has the ports you expect in a full-feature laptop.
L164:   * Full-size USB 3.0 port. Connect USB accessories—like a mouse, a printer, a 4G USB dongle, or an Ethernet adapter.
L165:   * microSD card slot. Use the microSD card slot for extra storage or transferring files. Learn more about cite80†Surface storage options . The microSD card slot is under the kickstand—it's shown in the cite81†Express yourself section.
L166:   * Mini DisplayPort version 1.2. Share what's on your Surface display by connecting it to an HDTV, monitor, or projector (video adapters sold separately). Learn more about cite82†Connect Surface to an external display .
L167:   * Charging port and 36-watt power supply. Attach the included power supply to the charging port when your battery is low. Learn more about cite83†How to charge Surface .
L168:   * Cover port. Add Type Cover for Surface Pro 3 (sold separately) so you'll always have a keyboard with you. Thin and light, Type Cover even helps protect your touchscreen while you're on the go. For more info, see cite84†Surface Type Cover .
L169: ### Next-gen power for your ideas
L170: 
L171: Surface Pro 3 uses the 4th generation Intel® Core™ processor (i3, i5 or i7). Choose from one of the following:
L172: 
L173:   * 64GB or 128GB storage with 4GB RAM
L174:   * 256GB or 512GB storage with 8GB RAM
L175: 
L176: Want even more storage? Learn more about cite80†Surface storage options .
L177: ### Express yourself
L178: 
L179: Music, podcasts, and audio books sound crisp and clear with Dolby® enhanced sound. Need a bit more privacy? Plug your favorite headset into the headset jack. When you’re on your next call or making your next video, take advantage of the noise-cancelling stereo headphones. See cite85†Surface sound, volume, and audio accessories for more info.
L180: And for both videos and photos, you’ll have two 5-megapixel cameras. Each camera has a privacy light, so there are no surprises. For more info, see cite86†Take photos and videos with Surface .
