L472: What games can I play on XBOX Series S?
L473: XBOX Series S is compatible with thousands of games across four generations of XBOX. And, with Smart Delivery games, you buy a digital game once and get the best version of that game for the console you’re playing on. Because XBOX Series S is all digital, access your digital game library through the console and select the game you want to play. Shop for games directly from your XBOX Series S console or online at cite67†XBOX.com .
L474: 
L475: Can you play XBOX One games on XBOX Series S?
L476: Yes. The digital titles that you own and are part of the Back Compat game catalog will automatically show up in the “Ready to Install” section on your XBOX Series S console. Disc-based games are not supported. cite68†Browse back compat games .

L478: Will XBOX Series S play game discs?
L479: 
L480: No. XBOX Series S consoles are all-digital gaming consoles for disc-free gaming. Disc-based games are not compatible with the 512GB or 1TB XBOX Series S consoles.
L481: 
L482: Do XBOX One controllers work on the Series S?
L483: XBOX Series X|S consoles are compatible with most XBOX One controllers and gaming accessories. cite69†Learn more†support.xbox.com .

L485: What’s included with XBOX Series S?
L486: 
L487: With XBOX Series S, an XBOX Series S console, controller, Ultra High Speed HDMI cable, and power cord are included. You can add standalone purchases of XBOX Game Pass and the Storage Expansion Cards for XBOX Series X|S for the complete experience.^{cite70†*** }
L488: 
L489: Does XBOX Series S deliver on speed and performance?
L490: Yes. XBOX Series S is powered by the XBOX Velocity Architecture, such as faster loading, the ability to seamlessly switch between multiple games with Quick Resume, richer and more dynamic worlds, and frame rates up to 120 FPS.
L491: 
L492: What’s the difference between XBOX Series S and XBOX Series X?
L493: Both XBOX Series X|S consoles are powered by the XBOX Velocity Architecture, such as faster loading, the ability to seamlessly switch between multiple games with Quick Resume, richer and more dynamic worlds, and frame rates up to 120 FPS.
L494: The primary difference between the two consoles is that the XBOX Series X displays games in native 4K, is available with a UHD Blu-ray Disc player, and you can choose between 1TB SSD or 2TB SSD. XBOX Series S is designed for disc-free gaming at 1440p (with the ability to upscale to 4K) and you can choose between 512GB SSD or 1TB SSD. And, of course, they're priced differently. Both consoles have the option to expand available custom storage via the Storage Expansion Card.
