L158: ## Surface Pro 4 features
L159: ### Express yourself
L160: 
L161: Enjoy a natural writing experience with a pen that feels like an actual pen. Use Surface Pen to launch Cortana (not available in all languages and countries/regions) or open OneNote and quickly jot down notes or take screenshots. Keep your pen handy with the convenient magnetic pen storage on the side of Surface Pro 4.
L162: Surface Pro 4 features two cameras for taking photos and recording video: an 8-megapixel rear-facing camera with autofocus and a 5-megapixel, high-resolution, front-facing camera. Both cameras record video in 1080p, with a 16:9 aspect ratio. Privacy lights are located on the right side of both cameras.
L163: 
L164: Surface Pro 4 also features an infrared (IR) face-detection camera so you can sign in to Windows without typing a password. For more info, see cite78†Windows Hello .
L165: For more camera info, see cite79†Take photos and videos with Surface .
L166: ### Work, play, watch, listen
L167: 
L168: Flip out the 150-degree-angle kickstand and work or play comfortably at your desk, on the couch, or while giving a hands-free presentation.
L169: 
L170: Stereo front firing speakers provide immersive music and movie playback experience and are 20 percent louder compared to Surface Pro 3.
L171: 
L172: To learn more, see cite80†Surface sound, volume, and audio accessories .
L173: ### Stay connected
L174: 
L175: Surface Pro 4 has the ports you expect in a full-feature laptop.
L176:   * Full-size USB 3.0 port
L177: Connect a USB accessory like a mouse, printer, Ethernet adapter, USB drive, or smartphone.
L178:   * Surface Connect for charging
L179: When your battery is low, attach the included power supply to the Surface Connect charging port. For more info, see cite81†How to charge Surface .
L180:   * MicroSD card slot
L181: Use the microSD card slot and a microSD card (sold separately) for file transfer and extra storage. For more info, see cite82†Find storage options for your Surface .
L182:   * Mini DisplayPort version 1.2
L183: Share what’s on your Surface screen by connecting it to an HDTV, monitor, or projector. (Video adapters are sold separately.)
L184:   * 3.5 mm headset jack
L185: Plug in your favorite headset for a little more privacy when listening to music or conference calls. For more info, see cite80†Surface sound, volume, and audio accessories .
L186:   * Cover connectors
L187: Click in the thin, light, Type Cover for Surface Pro 4 (sold separately) so you’ll always have a keyboard when you’re on the go. For more info, see cite83†Type Cover .
