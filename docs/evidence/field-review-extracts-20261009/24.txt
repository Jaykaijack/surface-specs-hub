L161: ## Surface Laptop Studio features
L162: ### Surface Laptop Studio adapts to you
L163: Surface Laptop Studio transforms from a laptop to an angled stage to a portable canvas, so you can get things done throughout your day. Start by using it like a laptop. When you're ready to collaborate with colleagues, watch a show online or play Xbox games online, pull the screen forward so it's angled in stage mode.
L164: When inspiration strikes, pull the screen forward all the way down, so it's the perfect height to use as a portable studio to write, draw, or just doodle with Surface Slim Pen 2 (sold separately). For more info, see cite81†Different ways to use Surface Laptop Studio .
L165: cite82†Image: Surface Laptop Studio in laptop mode, stage mode, and studio mode L166: ### Large Precision Haptic touchpad
L167: 
L168: Surface Laptop Studio has a large haptic touchpad that gives you realistic feedback when you tap it—you'll hear it click when you're using it. With its large size, you have more room to work. And because it runs on Windows 11, you can customize the touchpad gestures. For more info see, cite83†Surface touchpad use and settings .
L169: 
L170: cite84†Image: Large haptic touchpad on Surface Laptop Studio L171: ### Wake on Touch
L172: 
L173: When your Surface Laptop Studio goes to sleep, you can quickly wake it—just tap the screen when you're in stage or studio mode to get back to what you were doing.
L174: ### Surface Slim Pen 2 is the perfect companion for Surface Laptop Studio
L175: Surface Slim Pen 2 (sold separately) is made for Surface Laptop Studio. Just fold the screen down to use it in studio mode for a great, realistic writing and drawing experience. When drawing on the vibrant PixelSense^{TM} Flow 14.4" touch display that has a refresh rate up to 120Hz, you'll feel like you're drawing on paper. Whether you're drawing a masterpiece or just sketching things out, Surface Laptop Studio and Surface Slim Pen 2 will help you get it done when you want.
L176: When you're finished, just put Surface Slim Pen 2 under the front of the keyboard of Surface Laptop Studio—it attaches magnetically to securely store it and charge. For more info about Surface Slim Pen 2, see cite85†Use Surface Slim Pen 2 .
