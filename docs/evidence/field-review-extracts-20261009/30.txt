L164: ## Surface Book 3 features
L165: 
L166: ### Work your way, anywhere
L167: 
L168: Work your way with three versatile modes of use: Surface Book 3 is a robust laptop, powerful tablet, and portable studio in one.
L169: ### Unleash your best ideas
L170: 
L171: Surface Book 3 features the best graphics on any Surface laptop, powered by the latest NVIDIA®  GTX GeForce GPU, up to 2TB of storage, and up to 32GB RAM.
L172: 
L173: ### All the connections you need for multiple devices and accessories
L174: 
L175: Surface Book 3 comes with all the connections you need, including USB-A and versatile USB-C™ ports, plus a full-size SD card slot.
L176: ### Breathe new life into your creations
L177: 
L178: Use touch, Surface Pen, and Surface Dial on the vibrant PixelSense™ touchscreen display. Available in two sizes: 13.5" or 15".

L281: [1] Battery life:
L282: Surface Book 3 13.5” on base: Up to 15.5 hours of battery life based on typical Surface device usage. Testing conducted by Microsoft in April 2020 using preproduction software and preproduction configurations of Surface Book 3 13.5” Intel® Core™ i5, 256 GB, 8 GB RAM. Testing consisted of full battery discharge with a mixture of active use and modern standby.
L283: The active use portion consists of (1) a web browsing test accessing 8 popular websites over multiple open tabs, (2) a productivity test utilising Microsoft Word, PowerPoint, Excel and Outlook, and (3) a portion of time with the device in use with idle applications. All settings were default except screen brightness was set to 150 nits with Auto-Brightness disabled. Wi-Fi was connected to a network. Battery life varies significantly with settings, usage and other factors.
L284: Surface Book 3 15” on base: Up to 17.5 hours of battery life based on typical Surface device usage. Testing conducted by Microsoft in April 2020 using preproduction software and preproduction configurations of Surface Book 3 15” Intel® Core™ i7, 256 GB, 16 GB RAM. Testing consisted of full battery discharge with a mixture of active use and modern standby.
L285: The active use portion consists of (1) a web browsing test accessing 8 popular websites over multiple open tabs, (2) a productivity test utilising Microsoft Word, PowerPoint, Excel and Outlook, and (3) a portion of time with the device in use with idle applications. All settings were default except screen brightness was set to 150 nits with Auto-Brightness disabled. Wi-Fi was connected to a network. Battery life varies significantly with settings, usage and other factors.
L286: [2] System software uses significant storage space. Available storage is subject to change based on system software updates and apps usage. 1 GB = 1 Gigabyte. 1 TB = 1,000 GB. See cite87†Surface Storage for more details.
