L130:   * cite75†Explore the newest Surface PCs†www.microsoft.com L131: ## Get to know Surface Book (1st Gen)
L132: 
L133: cite76†Image: Surface Book viewed from the left front with callouts for its cameras, microphones, speakers, display, and left-side connections. L134: 
L135: Here's a quick overview of Surface Book features.
L136: 
L137: If you want to learn more about Surface Book, download the cite77†Surface Book User Guide†www.bing.com .
L138: Feature  | Details
L139: --- | ---
L140: Power button  | Press the power button to turn your Surface Book on. You can also use the power button to put your Surface Book to sleep and wake it when you're ready to start working again.
L141: Clipboard  | Detach the thin, light Clipboard from the keyboard for mobile computing and easy presentation in tablet mode.
L142: Use the 13.5 full HD display, with its 3:2 aspect ratio and 3000 x 2000 resolution display, to watch HD movies, browse the web, and use your favorite apps.
L143: The 10-point multi-touch touchscreen lets you use your fingers to select, zoom, and move things around.
L144: For more info, see cite78†Touch gestures for Windows .
L145: Keyboard  | Mechanical friction attaches the Clipboard to the keyboard. The keyboard features a standard layout and backlit keys, with special shortcut and function keys to help you do common tasks more quickly.
L146: Surface Pen  | Write naturally with the included Surface Pen (Surface Pro 4 version). Quickly jot down notes or capture screenshots in OneNote, even while your Surface is locked. See cite79†How to use your Surface Pen (Surface Pro 4 version) for more info.
L147: Wi-Fi and Bluetooth  | Surface Book supports standard Wi-Fi protocols (802.11a/b/g/n/ac) and Bluetooth^{®} 4.0. Connect to a wireless network and use Bluetooth devices such as mice, printers, and headsets.
L148: Cameras  | Surface Book features an 8-megapixel rear camera with autofocus and a 5-megapixel, fixed-focus front camera. Both cameras are high resolution and record video in 1080p, with a 16:9 aspect ratio (widescreen).
L149: For more info, see cite80†Take photos and videos with Surface .
L150: Surface Book is also Windows Hello compatible with an infrared (IR) face-detection camera built in. See cite81†Windows Hello for more info.
L151: Microphones  | Two noise-canceling microphones ensure accurate audio recording and enhanced call quality.
L152: Stereo speakers  | Two front-facing stereo speakers with Dolby^{®} enhanced sound provide an immersive playback experience for all your music, podcasts, and audio books.
L153: To learn more, see cite82†Surface sound, volume, and audio accessories .
L154: cite83†Image: Surface Book viewed from the right front with callouts for its cameras, buttons, touchscreen, Clipboard, keyboard, and right-side connections. L155: Feature  | Details
L156: --- | ---
L157: Ports and connectors  | Surface Book has the ports you expect from a full-feature laptop.
L158: Two full-size USB 3.0 ports
L159: Connect a USB accessory like a mouse, printer, Ethernet adapter, USB drive, or smartphone.
L160: SD card slot
L161: Use the full-size SDXC (UHS-I / UHS-II) card slot with an SD card (sold separately) for extra storage and transferring files. For more info, see cite84†Surface storage options .
L162: Mini DisplayPort version 1.2a
L163: Share what's on your Surface screen by connecting it to an HDTV, monitor, or projector. (Video adapters are sold separately.) For more info, see cite85†Connect Surface to an external display .
L164: 3.5 mm headset jack
L165: Plug in your favorite headset for a little more privacy when listening to music or conference calls. For more info, see cite82†Surface sound, volume, and audio accessories .
L166: Software  | Windows 10 operating system
L167: Windows 10 provides a variety of options for entertainment and productivity whether you're at school, at home, or on the go.
L168: To learn about the latest features in Windows, see cite86†What's new in recent Windows 10 updates .
L169: Apps
L170: Surface Book comes with great built-in apps, and you can find more apps in cite87†Microsoft Store .
L171: Processor  | The 6th Gen Intel® Core i5 or i7 processor provides the speed and power you need for smooth, fast performance.
L172: Memory and storage  | Choose from 8 GB of RAM with 128 GB storage to 16 GB of RAM with 512 GB storage.
L173: See cite88†Surface storage for details about formatted storage capacity.
L174: For info on other storage options for Surface Book, see cite84†Surface storage options .
L175: Sensors  | Six sensors—compass, ambient light sensor, accelerometer, gyroscope, Hall effect, and Wi-Fi SAR—let apps do things like track motion and determine location.
