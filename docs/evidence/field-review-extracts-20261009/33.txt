L167: ## Surface Go 2 features
L168: 
L169: ### Connect your device anywhere
L170: 
L171: Get laptop versatility with the built-in Kickstand and Signature Type Cover. With WiFi and optional LTE Advanced, you’ll be able to work anywhere you need to.
L172: ### Performance from day to night
L173: 
L174: Run Office 365, apps, Netflix, and more with the Intel® Pentium® Gold and Pentium® M processors available for Surface Go 2.
L175: 
L176: ### Browse and play on your PixelSense™ Display
L177: 
L178: Your screen has a higher resolution on its 10.5 inch display. The PixelSense Display makes your movies and shows look great, while the built in Dolby® Audio makes it sound great too.
L179: ### Seamless, safe, and secure
L180: 
L181: Sharing Surface Go 2 with your family is easy and safe with multiple-user logins, screen time limits, and content filtering. You’ll have space for your photos, documents, and other files on Surface Go 2. If you need more storage, use an SD card or get 5 GB of free storage of OneDrive with a Microsoft account.

L246: [1] Surface Go 2 battery life
L247: Surface Go 2: Up to 10 hours of battery life based on typical Surface device usage. Testing conducted by Microsoft in February 2020 using preproduction software and preproduction configurations of Surface Go 2. Testing consisted of full battery discharge with a mixture of active use and modern standby.
L248: The active use portion consists of (1) a web browsing test accessing 8 popular websites over multiple open tabs, (2) a productivity test utilizing Microsoft Word, PowerPoint, Excel and Outlook, and (3) a portion of time with the device in use with idle applications. All settings were default except screen brightness was set to 150 nits with Auto-Brightness disabled. Wi-Fi was connected to a network. Battery life varies significantly with settings, usage, and other factors.
L249: Surface Go 2 with 4G Advanced: Up to 10 hours of battery life based on typical Surface device usage. Testing conducted by Microsoft in February 2020 using preproduction software and preproduction configurations of Surface Go 2. Testing consisted of full battery discharge with a mixture of active use and modern standby.
L250: The active use portion consists of (1) a web browsing test accessing 8 popular websites over multiple open tabs, (2) a productivity test utilizing Microsoft Word, PowerPoint, Excel and Outlook, and (3) a portion of time with the device in use with idle applications.
