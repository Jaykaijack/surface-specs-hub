L162:   * Sleek and light weight touchscreen laptop with a comfortable keyboard.
L163:   * Choose the 13.5” PixelSense™ touchscreen for portable productivity, or larger 15” for split-screen multitasking. In warm Alcantara® (13.5” touchscreen only) or cool metal, and new colors including Sage.^{1}
L164:   * Surface Laptop 5 is made for multitasking across multiple tabs and apps with powerful 12th Gen Intel® Core™ i5/i7 processors built on the Intel® Evo™ platform.
L165:   * Versatile Thunderbolt™ 4 connectivity and reliable all-day battery.^{2}
L166:   * Look and sound your best on calls with Studio Mics and enhanced camera experiences, powered by Windows 11.
L167:   * Ultra-vivid colors with Dolby Vision®^{3} and sound that moves around you with Dolby Atmos®.^{4}
L168:   * Peace of mind with Windows Hello sign-in, built-in Windows 11 security, and secured OneDrive cloud storage for your Microsoft 365 files.^{5}

L174:   3. Requires Dolby Vision® encoded content and video.
L175:   4. Requires Dolby Atmos® encoded content and audio.
